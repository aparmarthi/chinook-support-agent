The experiment

Both arms resolved 30/30. I shipped flat.

Bars were pre-registered before the supervisor existed. It came in +2.8s on p50 against a +2.0s ceiling, with no mixed-intent gain and no quality gain to pay for it.

Last week this said 30 against 29, in the supervisor's favour, and I shipped flat anyway — one example on thirty isn't a quality difference. Rerun at this commit, they tie. That is what a one-example margin was worth. What reproduced both times is the overhead of the extra hop: 1.7x median latency, 61 tool calls against 45. The verdict rests on that.
