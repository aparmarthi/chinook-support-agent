What made the decision credible

The first controlled freeze showed 29–30. I did not bank a one-example lead.

The bar was written before the challenger existed. After the escalation fix, both arms were rerun at the same commit and tied. What survived was the extra hop: 1.7× median latency and 16 more tool calls.

Decision rule: visible quality gain + no workflow regression + ≤2.0s latency penalty. Ties go to the simpler topology.
